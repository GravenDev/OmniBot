# Create and manage your temporary voice channel

This guide shows you how to get your own temporary voice channel and set it up from its control panel.

Before you start: an administrator has enabled the **Temporary Voice Channels** module and set at least one hub with `/config temp-voice`.

1. Join the hub voice channel. The bot creates your channel, moves you into it and posts a control panel in its text chat.
2. To choose who can join, open the panel's **Who can join?** menu and pick **Open**, **Invite only** or **Private**.
3. To rename the channel or limit the number of members, click **Name and limit**, fill in the form and submit.
4. To let a friend in when the channel is invite only or private, click **Members**, then pick them in the **Invite** menu. To let them know, then click **Let them know by DM**.
5. To stop someone from seeing or joining the channel, click **Members**, then pick them in the **Block** menu.
6. To give the channel to another member in it, click **Transfer** and pick them.
7. Leave the channel when you are done. It is deleted as soon as it is empty.

Each action shows a message visible to you only that confirms the change, and the panel updates. Your name, limit, access mode and lists carry over to your next channel.

Every button and menu of the panel is described in the [module reference](../reference/modules/temp-voice).
